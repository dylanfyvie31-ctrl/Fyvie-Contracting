What we do

Done properly, first time.

Yard works for Perth homeowners — turf, retic, paving prep, garden makeovers and full clean-ups. Hands-on, local, and always tidied up after.

01

Turf Preparation & Install

Old lawn or weeds stripped out, soil graded and screened, then fresh turf laid, rolled and watered in. A lawn that takes — not one you'll be replacing next summer.

02

Concrete & Paving Prep

Full base prep for new slabs, paths, driveways and paved areas. Dig-out, screened road base, levelled and compacted — ready for your concreter or paver to lay straight onto.

03

Site Clean-Up

Builders' rubble, green waste, old paths, broken slabs and yard junk loaded out and carted off — tipper hired in to suit the load. Block left clean and ready for the next stage.

04

Retic Install

Reticulation for lawns and garden beds — trenched in, drip lines, pop-ups, solenoids and controllers. Plumbed, programmed and tested before we leave site.

05

Garden & Landscaping

Garden bed builds, mulching, soil top-ups, plant-outs and full backyard makeovers. Tired yards turned into something you actually want to use.

06

Pool Removal & Digs

Old fibreglass and concrete pools cut up, dug out and carted away. Hole back-filled, compacted and graded — ready for turf, paving or a fresh build. Pool digs for new installs done the same way.

About

A new Perth business, built on straight work and a straight answer.

Fyvie Contracting is a new, locally-owned landscaping business servicing Perth homeowners across the metro.
